## Hi, I'm Maciej 👋

Founder of **Jobbly** — a workforce management platform for small businesses.

---

### 🚀 What I'm Working On

I'm building **Jobbly**, a modern workforce management platform designed to help small businesses manage their teams with less chaos and more clarity.

Jobbly is being built as a connected workforce management product, with distinct experiences designed for business owners, managers, and employees — all working together around the same organisation and team data.

The product focuses on practical day-to-day business operations, including scheduling, team structure, communication, availability, and operational workflows.

The goal is simple: help business owners and managers save time, stay organised, and run their teams more confidently.
